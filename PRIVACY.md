## What this app is

Order from your table over the room's own Wi-Fi — a LAN canteen board built with Forge.

## What it collects

**Nothing.** No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no fingerprinting, no advertising identifier and
no email capture.

It talks to **a server on your own network** — the one you point it at — and to nothing else. What you type goes to that machine and stays on your LAN; it does not pass through the internet and the author of this app never sees it. If that server is not yours, whoever runs it can read what you send.

## What is stored on your device

Whatever the app remembers between visits stays on this device. Clearing the app's stored data erases it.

## Links that leave the app

Documentation and support pages contain ordinary links. Opening one takes you to that site, with its own
privacy policy; the app loads nothing from it.

## Erasing

Clear the app's stored data, or delete the app itself. Nothing is held anywhere else, so there is no other
copy to ask us to remove.

## Changes

If a future version ever adds a request that carries your data, this file changes in the same commit.
